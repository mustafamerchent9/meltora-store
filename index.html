    Frequently Asked Questions

    What is the difference between cheddar and mozzarella?

    Cheddar is a firm cheese with a fuller, sharper flavour that suits burgers, sandwiches and grating. Mozzarella is milder and is known for stretching when melted, which is why it is popular on pizza and in baked pasta.

    Which cheese is suitable for pizza?

    Mozzarella is the classic choice for pizza because it melts and stretches well. Many people also blend in a little cheddar for extra flavour.
